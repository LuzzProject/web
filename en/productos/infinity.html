A precise, minimalist light installation, activated and transformed through projection mapping. The visuals—tracing the curve of the tracks—create the illusion of continuity and motion.

Features a structure of ten interlacing white tracks that form nested infinity loops. Laser-cut black acrylic strips frame each track.
